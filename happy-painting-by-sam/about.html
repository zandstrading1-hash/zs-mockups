About

One painter who knows your project.

Happy Painting by Sam is a residential and commercial painting company based in Windsor, Ontario. Sam runs every job personally. He's the one who comes out to look at your space, gives you an honest quote and makes sure the final coat is right, with clear communication the whole way through.

That's the idea behind the name: the job isn't finished until you're happy with it.
